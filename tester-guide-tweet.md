# TipWall Beta Tester Guide — Tweet Thread

## Tweet 1/5 — The invitation

🚀 TipWall is live for beta testing!

TipWall is a public support wall inside Nimiq Pay. Supporters tip creators directly in NIM (and soon USDT0 on Polygon). Zero platform fees. Fully on-chain.

Here's how to test it 👇

## Tweet 2/5 — Set up Nimiq Pay

1️⃣ Download **Nimiq Pay** from the App Store / Play Store
2️⃣ Create a wallet and **write down your 24-word recovery phrase** on paper
3️⃣ Get some NIM:
   • Tap "Get NIM" in the app to buy with card/bank
   • Or ask a friend to send you 50-100 NIM
   • Or check community faucets

That's it. You're ready.

## Tweet 3/5 — Open TipWall

TipWall is a **mini app inside Nimiq Pay** — not a standalone site.

→ In Nimiq Pay, go to Mini Apps and find **TipWall**
→ Or open directly: https://tipwall.vercel.app

It loads inside Nimiq Pay, so your wallet is already connected. No extra sign-ins.

## Tweet 4/5 — Test the flows

Try these:

✅ Create your own wall (pick a handle, set a goal)
✅ Tip someone in **NIM** — pick an amount, confirm, watch it verify on-chain
✅ Tip in **USDT0 on Polygon** — switch chains, confirm, paste the tx hash
✅ Share your wall — copy the link, post it, check the preview card

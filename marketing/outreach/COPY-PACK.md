if it isn't, use the `[NO-USDT]` variants and drop every money claim.

**Rules for all outreach:**
1. Never send a bare link. Always offer to walk them through it.
2. Always append `?ref=<channel>` to your links — `dm`, `discord`, `x`, `badge`, `telegram`.
3. Never open a non-web3 conversation with the word *crypto*.

## 9. Objection handling

**"How do I get NIM?"**
> Two clicks — there's a "New to NIM?" link in the create flow that takes you to
> Nimiq's buy page. Or skip it entirely and take USDT instead. Happy to walk you
> through either right now.

**"Crypto is a scam / too volatile."**
> Fair. That's why I never hold your money — tips go straight to your wallet and
> TipWall can't touch them. And there's a stablecoin option (USDT) for anyone who
> doesn't want price risk. The non-custodial part is the whole point: there's no
> company that can lose your funds.

**"Why not just put a wallet address in my bio?"**
> You can, and it's equally fee-free — that's my real competitor. The wall adds
> three things an address can't: people tell you *why*, the record is permanent
> and public, and you get discovered in the directory. If all you want is an
> address, use an address.

**"No one will tip me."**
> Possibly. That's the honest answer — this works if you have people who already
> value your work. The good news is a tip costs your supporters almost nothing,
> so the barrier is on their side too. Set it up, share it once, see what happens.

**"What if TipWall shuts down?"**
> Then you still have your money — it was never ours. And you can export a
> signed snapshot of your whole wall: canonical JSON, Ed25519 signature,
> verifiable by anyone. Your history doesn't depend on us existing.

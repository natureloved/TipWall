# TipWall Beta Tester Guide

## Step 1: Get the Nimiq Pay app

1. Open your phone's app store (iOS App Store or Google Play).
2. Search for **"Nimiq Pay"** and install the official app.
3. Open the app and follow the onboarding to create a new wallet.
4. **Back up your 24-word recovery phrase** on paper. This is your only way to recover your wallet. Never screenshot it or store it digitally.

## Step 2: Get some NIM

You need NIM (Nimiq's native currency) to test tipping. Here's how to get it:

### Option A: Buy NIM directly
- In the Nimiq Pay app, tap **"Get NIM"** or the **buy** button.
- Follow the flow to purchase with a card or bank transfer via the integrated provider.
- You only need a small amount (e.g., 50-100 NIM) for testing.

### Option B: Receive NIM from a friend
- Share your Nimiq address (found on your wallet's home screen) with someone who already has NIM.
- Ask them to send you 50-100 NIM.

### Option C: Faucet (if available)
- Check the Nimiq community Discord or Telegram for active testnet/mainnet faucets.
- Some community members run small faucets for new users.

## Step 3: Open TipWall inside Nimiq Pay

TipWall is a **Nimiq Pay mini app** — it runs inside the Nimiq Pay app, not as a standalone website.

1. In the Nimiq Pay app, look for the **"Mini Apps"** or **"Explore"** section.
2. Find **TipWall** in the mini app directory, or use the direct link:
   `https://tipwall.vercel.app`
3. Tap to open TipWall. It will load inside Nimiq Pay's built-in browser.

## Step 4: Create your own wall (optional but recommended)

1. On the TipWall home screen, tap **"Create your wall"**.
2. Choose a unique **handle** (e.g., `@yourname`).
3. Add a display name, bio, and optionally a goal.
4. **Connect your wallet** when prompted — this verifies you own the wall.
5. Your wall is now live at `https://tipwall.vercel.app/yourname`

## Step 5: Test tipping

### Test A: Tip with NIM
1. Visit any wall (yours or someone else's).
2. Tap the **"Send tip"** button.
3. Choose **NIM** as the payment asset.
4. Enter an amount (e.g., 10 NIM) and optionally a message.
5. Confirm the transaction in Nimiq Pay.
6. Wait a few seconds for verification — your tip should appear on the wall!

### Test B: Tip with USDT0 on Polygon (if the wall supports it)
1. Visit a wall that has a Polygon address set (look for the USDT0 tab).
2. Tap **"Send tip"** and switch to **USDT0**.
3. If your wallet is on the wrong chain, TipWall will prompt you to switch to Polygon.
4. Confirm the transaction in your wallet.
5. Paste the transaction hash when prompted so TipWall can verify it.

## Step 6: Test sharing

1. After creating your wall, tap **"Share"**.
2. Try sharing to X (Twitter), Telegram, or copying the link.
3. Paste the link somewhere and check that the preview card shows correctly with the new banner and logo.

## What to look for / Report

- Does the logo show correctly everywhere (tab icon, share preview, app icon)?
- Does the banner show in share previews (X, Telegram, WhatsApp)?
- Does the NIM tipping flow feel smooth?
- Does the USDT0 tab appear for walls with a Polygon address?
- Any errors, confusing UI, or steps that feel broken?

Report bugs or feedback in the replies to this tweet or in our community chat.
